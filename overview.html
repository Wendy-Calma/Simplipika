Project Overview

Simplipika

A project that serves as a solution for a lack of data-driven solutions in Barangay Services. It focuses on four services: Blotter/Incident Reporting, Social Assistance Program, Resident Registration and Management, and Certificate Request and Issuance. It integrates an email notification service and online payment (simulated) service.

Key Features

  • Blotter/Incident Reporting Service
  • Social Assistance Program Service
  • Resident Registration and Management Service
  • Certificate Request and Issuance Service
  • Automated Notifications via Email
  • Online Payment Simulation
